\subsubsection{Implementation and Computing Environment}\label{sec:exp-implementation}

\hpupdated{Model experiments used a single NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB GPU. Table~\ref{tab:hyperparameter-implementation} summarizes the hardware, software, and training settings for the hyperparameter experiments. All configurations in the hyperparameter sweep used the same data split, with a maximum of 200 training epochs and an early-stopping patience of 20 epochs. We selected the checkpoint with the lowest joint validation MAE and reported both MAE and RMSE at that checkpoint.}
